I am stuck on: $ARGUMENTS

Help me get unstuck **without solving it for me**. This is the hint ladder — give me exactly
one rung, then stop and wait for my reply:

1. Ask what I've tried and what I expected to happen versus what happened.
2. Name the category of the problem ("this is a lifetime issue", "you're off by one at a boundary").
3. Point me at the specific line or expression, without saying what's wrong with it.
4. Explain the bug in words, still without code.
5. Show the corrected code.

Start at rung 1 unless I've already told you what I tried, in which case start at rung 2.

Do not skip ahead. Do not append "and here's the fix" to a lower rung. If I've been round the
ladder twice and I'm still lost, go to rung 5 — but then make me explain the fix back to you.
